# Weekly workflow

This is the intended operating process. Automation described here is not implemented yet.

## 1. Open the week

Choose the Sunday date and confirm whether morning and evening services are scheduled. Review recent service logs for continuity and song repetition. Locate the authoritative planning workbook and the relevant Drive folders.

## 2. Plan both services

Record sermon title, text and topic; call to worship; prayer of confession; ordered praise songs and hymns; special music; and announcements. Identify the exact chord arrangements and keys. Mark unknown values as unresolved rather than inventing them.

Record which word study handouts and sermon outlines are needed for each service. AI can help draft and review language using supplied sources. A person checks Scripture references, quotations, theology and meaning.

## 3. Prepare congregational care separately

Review private prayer reports, including reports received through the private Facebook group. Retain authorized reports in the restricted Drive location with source/date and follow-up status. Confirm wording and the intended audience for the prayer bulletin. Private-group membership alone does not authorize wider publication.

Private prayer content never flows automatically into public website, social or broadcast text. Any public wording must be independently selected and approved for that audience.

## 4. Validate and prepare the snapshot

The planned Python import reads the selected workbook and reference libraries, checks dates and required fields, resolves referenced files, and writes a shared weekly snapshot in Drive. Report unresolved references before generating affected outputs. The input mapping will be based on real workbook structure.

## 5. Produce review copies

| Service or area | Review copies |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | Bulletin, praise chord packet, word study handout, sermon outline |
| Evening, when scheduled | Bulletin, praise chord packet, word study handout, sermon outline |
| Private prayer | Prayer bulletin through the separate restricted workflow |
| Digital | Coming this Sunday, public social copy, broadcast title/description, handout links |

Use the same weekly snapshot for shared facts. Retrieve existing chord files by verified reference. Keep substantial handout content in its own Drive documents. Future document generation must preserve usable print layouts.

## 6. Review and approve

Check date, service, sermon title/text, song order, keys, names, spelling, page breaks, legibility and audience. Compare shared facts across outputs. Review the rendered document, not just extracted text. Save approved versions separately and record approval per artifact.

Changes return affected outputs to draft and require another review. A week is not fully approved merely because one bulletin is approved.

## 7. Deliver and publish

Print/distribute the approved service materials. Manually publish reviewed website and public Facebook content. Use the separate private channel for prayer reports. Prepare Facebook Live and YouTube metadata and record the actual recording links after broadcast.

The existing Kenton website implementation remains a separate system. Begin by preparing content for it; define an integration only after its workflow is understood.

## 8. Close and archive

Record what actually occurred, including song substitutions, canceled services, updated handouts and recording links. Update the historical log once using the agreed format. Archive final delivered artifacts and the associated manifest. Prepare Where we've been from this actual record, rather than the earlier plan.

Keep private prayer history in its restricted archive. Record corrections without silently overwriting the previous public record.

## Exceptions

If source files are unavailable, leave the affected output unresolved. If a service is canceled, suppress its outputs explicitly. If a late correction arrives after publication, update the relevant destination and record the correction and time.
